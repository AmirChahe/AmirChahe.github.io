I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at Drexel University, advised by [Prof. Lifeng Zhou](https://zhourobotics.github.io/). My research is about building autonomous systems that **predict the consequences of their actions and plan with them**, with a focus on autonomous driving and robot navigation.

My recent work develops **latent world models** whose planning costs are shaped for sampling-based model predictive control. These models achieve state-of-the-art image-goal navigation and run zero-shot on a Clearpath Husky with onboard NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin inference. I also study how **vision-language models** can reason about driving scenes: identifying planning-critical occluded agents, answering driving questions with small reasoning-enhanced models, and querying 3D scenes with open vocabulary. Earlier, I showed how dynamic adversarial patches can manipulate the decisions of an autonomous vehicle in the real world.

I have spent two summers as a research intern at [Honda Research Institute USA](https://usa.honda-ri.com/). Before Drexel, I received my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Tehran.

**Research interests:** world models and model-based planning, vision-language-action (VLA) policies, closed-loop training and evaluation, safe and robust autonomy for driving and navigation, and efficient embodied inference.
